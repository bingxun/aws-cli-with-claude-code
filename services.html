What we do

Six practice areas that between them cover the full statutory and financial life of a Singapore private limited company.

Practice 01

Company incorporation

We register your Singapore private limited company end to end — name application with ACRA, a constitution drafted for your shareholding, first board resolutions, share certificates and the opening entries in your statutory registers. Straightforward incorporations complete in one to three working days once KYC clears.

Foreign founders get a package that solves the two things that actually block them: a resident director, and a bank that will open the account. We act as nominee director subject to KYC, and introduce you to the corporate banking desks we work with regularly.

  • Name reservation and constitution drafting
  • ACRA registration and business profile
  • First board resolutions and share certificates
  • Corporate bank account introduction
  • Nominee resident director where required
  • Employment Pass and EntrePass applications

Practice 02

Corporate secretarial

Every Singapore company must appoint a company secretary within six months of incorporation, and that person must be ordinarily resident here. In a private company with a sole director, the director cannot also fill the role. We supply a named, qualified secretary — a person you can call, not a mailbox.

The retainer covers the statutory rhythm: AGM documentation, the annual return, the register of registrable controllers, and the maintenance of your statutory registers. Corporate actions — share allotments, transfers, director changes, constitutional amendments — are handled as they arise and priced per event.

  • Named company secretary appointed to your company
  • Statutory registers and register of controllers
  • AGM documentation and annual return filing
  • Directors’ and shareholders’ resolutions
  • Share allotments, transfers and capital changes
  • Free transfer of your existing secretarial records

Practice 03

Accounting & bookkeeping

Books kept monthly, in the cloud, reconciled to the bank. We work in Xero and QuickBooks with live bank feeds and receipt capture, so your position is current rather than reconstructed in a panic each January.

At year end we prepare unaudited financial statements compliant with the Singapore Financial Reporting Standards, together with the XBRL filing ACRA requires. If your company crosses the audit threshold we prepare the schedules and manage the auditor relationship on your behalf.

  • Monthly or quarterly bookkeeping
  • Bank, credit card and payment gateway reconciliation
  • Accounts payable and receivable ledgers
  • Management reports with commentary
  • Unaudited financial statements and XBRL
  • Consolidated accounts for group structures

Practice 04

Corporate tax & GST

Singapore taxes corporate profits at a headline 17%, but very few young companies pay that. The Start-Up Tax Exemption and Partial Tax Exemption schemes materially reduce the bill, and we claim whichever applies as part of the computation rather than as an upsell.

Estimated Chargeable Income falls due within three months of your financial year end; Form C-S or Form C follows annually. On the GST side we advise on whether the S$1 million registration threshold applies to you, register you when it does, and file your F5 returns each quarter.

  • Estimated Chargeable Income filing
  • Tax computation with exemption claims
  • Form C-S and Form C submission
  • GST registration, de-registration and F5 returns
  • Withholding tax and Certificate of Residence
  • IRAS queries and audit representation

See tax fees

Practice 05

Payroll & HR

Payroll is the compliance area where mistakes are most visible to your staff. We run it on a fixed calendar: payslips issued on schedule, CPF submitted by the 14th, and the year-end IR8A forms filed under the Auto-Inclusion Scheme before the 1 March deadline.

Employing foreign staff adds work passes and tax clearance to the picture. We handle Employment Pass applications and renewals with MOM, and IR21 clearance when someone leaves Singapore.

  • Monthly payroll runs and digital payslips
  • CPF submission and GIRO instruction
  • IR8A and Auto-Inclusion Scheme filing
  • IR21 tax clearance for departing foreign staff
  • Leave and claims management
  • Employment contracts and work pass applications

See payroll fees

Practice 06

Audit & CFO advisory

A private company is exempt from audit when it meets at least two of three criteria in each of its last two financial years: revenue no more than S$10 million, total assets no more than S$10 million, and no more than 50 employees. We track where you sit against that test and tell you before you cross it.

When an audit is required, we prepare the schedules, answer the auditor’s queries and keep the timetable. And for companies that need finance leadership without a full-time hire, our fractional CFO engagement covers budgeting, cash-flow forecasting, board reporting and fundraising diligence.

  • Audit readiness assessment and schedules
  • Liaison with your appointed auditor
  • Budgeting and cash-flow forecasting
  • Board and investor reporting packs
  • Due diligence and data room support
  • Entity restructuring and strike-off
