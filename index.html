Frequently asked questions

Does every Singapore company need a company secretary?

Yes. Under the Companies Act, every Singapore company must appoint a company secretary within six months of incorporation, and the role cannot be left vacant for more than six months. The secretary must be ordinarily resident in Singapore, and for a private company the sole director cannot also act as the secretary.

What does it cost to switch corporate secretary to Minerva?

Nothing. We prepare the resignation and appointment resolutions, file the change with ACRA, and take custody of your statutory registers at no charge when you engage us on any annual package.

When do I need to register for GST?

Registration is compulsory once your taxable turnover exceeds S$1 million in a calendar year, or when you reasonably expect it to. Voluntary registration is possible below that threshold and often makes sense for exporters and businesses with heavy input tax. We will tell you which side of the line you are on before you commit.

Do you handle companies that are behind on their filings?

Frequently. Late annual returns, unfiled ECI and overdue AGMs are all recoverable. We scope the backlog during onboarding, quote the catch-up work separately from your ongoing fee, and deal with ACRA and IRAS on your behalf.

Does my company need an audit?

Only if it fails the small company test. A private company is exempt when it meets at least two of three criteria in each of the last two financial years: revenue no more than S$10 million, total assets no more than S$10 million, and no more than 50 employees. If you do need an audit, we prepare the schedules and manage the auditor relationship for you.

Can you act as my nominee resident director?

Yes, subject to KYC and a refundable security deposit. Every Singapore company needs at least one director who is ordinarily resident here — a citizen, permanent resident, or EntrePass/Employment Pass holder. Our nominee director service is priced on the fee schedule.
