A firm built around the client, not the timesheet

Minerva Strategies was founded on a simple observation: most small companies do not want more accounting advice. They want to stop worrying about it.

Why we started

Between us, our partners have spent years inside Big Four practices and inside the finance functions of the companies those practices serve. Both vantage points make the same problem obvious. Compliance work is largely predictable — the deadlines are known years in advance, the forms rarely change — yet it is still sold as though every hour were a discovery.

So we priced it the way it actually behaves. Published fees. A named person on your account. Deadlines tracked on our side, not yours. If a piece of work genuinely cannot be quoted in advance, we say so and scope it before we start.

The name comes from Minerva, the Roman goddess of wisdom and strategy, and the eight-pointed compass in our mark is the reason we chose her: our job is to keep you oriented, whichever direction your business turns.

How we work

Transparency first

Our entire fee schedule is published on this website. You can price an engagement without speaking to a salesperson, and nothing appears on an invoice that was not agreed first.

People, not portals

Software helps, but it does not answer questions about a share transfer at 9pm. Every client has a named accountant and a review manager, both reachable directly.

Proactive, not reactive

We prompt you ahead of every statutory deadline and flag issues — an approaching GST threshold, a looming audit requirement — before they become urgent.

Specialists in Singapore

We do one jurisdiction and we do it properly. ACRA, IRAS, MOM and CPF are the four bodies we deal with daily, and we know how each of them actually operates.

Clean handovers

If you leave, your records go with you promptly and without charge. A client who stays because switching is painful is not a client relationship worth having.

Independent by design

We do not take commissions from software vendors or banks. When we recommend a tool or an auditor, it is because it fits your company.
