Pricing questions

Are there any charges not shown on this page?

Statutory fees payable to ACRA, IRAS and MOM are not ours to set, so they are passed through at cost and itemised separately. Beyond that, if a piece of work is not on this schedule we quote it in writing before starting. You will not receive an invoice for something you did not approve.

What happens if my transaction volume grows mid-year?

We review volume quarterly. If you move into a higher band we tell you at the review and the new rate applies from the following month — never retroactively.

Can I pay monthly instead of annually?

Yes. Monthly billing is available on all packages at a 10% premium to cover the administration. Annual billing in advance is the published rate.

Is there a minimum contract term?

Packages run for twelve months. If you leave mid-term we refund the unused months on a pro-rata basis, less any work already delivered, and we release your statutory registers to your new agent without charge.

Not sure which package fits?

Tell us your entity type, monthly transaction volume and headcount. We will point you at the right tier — including telling you when the cheaper one is enough.
