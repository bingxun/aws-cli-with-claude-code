Before you write to us

How quickly will I hear back?

Within one business day for enquiries received during office hours. Incorporation enquiries usually get a same-day response because the timeline matters to you.

Will I be quoted on the call, or afterwards?

Afterwards, in writing. We would rather spend twenty minutes understanding your entity than give you a number on the spot that we later have to revise.

Do you take clients outside Singapore?

We act for Singapore-registered entities, wherever their owners live. A large share of our clients are foreign founders who have never set foot in the office.

What do you need from me to start?

Identity and address documents for each director and shareholder holding 25% or more, your existing statutory registers if you have them, and access to your accounting system. We send a checklist after the scoping call.
